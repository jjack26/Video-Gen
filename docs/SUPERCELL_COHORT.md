## Findings and what they do not establish

The selected short character-premise clips have more observed views than the selected podcast excerpt and card reveal. This is a useful selection signal for the next visual review. It is not a matched experiment: upload ages, topic appeal, distribution, thumbnail/title effects and promotion can differ. Do not infer that every short clip or every emotional story will outperform explanations.

The two April narrative films differ dramatically in cumulative views despite coming from the same official channel and being released five days apart. This rules out treating production polish or the series name as enough to select a winner. The data does not identify whether story, thumbnail, promotion or another cause explains the gap. Both films still require visual and audio review before borrowing any structural lesson.

The current priority is specific character behavior and visual storytelling rather than a generic rage label. That is an account-specific creative direction supported by user feedback and worth testing, not a proven algorithm rule. A new fan account has neither the official channel’s audience nor its campaign distribution.
